Project Abstract

Revolutionizing Single-Cell Analysis

The -BARC project introduces a paradigm shift in cellular phenotyping. By marrying integrated whispering-gallery mode microlasers with high-frequency acoustic sensing, we encode mechanical properties of individual cells into distinct optical barcodes without altering cellular viability.

Microlaser Photonics

High-Q optical microcavities generate ultra-narrow spectral shifts responsive to minute mechanical and acoustic impedance changes in fluidic pathways.

Mechanical Barcoding

Assigning optical-acoustic identity tags to individual cells, enabling real-time multiplexed tracking in heterogeneous tumor and stem cell populations.

Acoustofluidics

Advanced lab-on-a-chip architecture manipulating micro-particles with acoustic waves for label-free, high-throughput diagnostic screening.

Objectives & Methodology

Bridging Photonics with Cellular Mechanics

Traditional cellular analysis relies heavily on fluorescent labels that can interfere with normal cell physiology. -BARC overcomes this barrier through an interdisciplinary approach combining optics, acoustics, and nanotechnology.

1

Label-Free Mechanics

Probing elasticity and viscosity without chemical staining or genetic modification.

2

Sub-Micron Resolution

Fabricating whispering-gallery mode sensors with nanometer precision.

3

AI-Driven Spectral Decoding

Real-time machine learning algorithms decoding acoustic resonance signatures instantly.

Research Pillars

Work Packages & Project Structure

Explore the core technical work packages driving the -BARC project lifecycle.

Work Package 1 • Months 1-18

Advanced Microlaser Fabrication & Cavity Optimization

Design and nanofabrication of high-Q whispering-gallery mode (WGM) microlasers on silicon-on-insulator (SOI) and polymer substrates. This pillar focuses on optimizing resonance linewidths and tuning emission spectra to maximize sensitivity against acoustic pressure variations.

Lead Beneficiary Photonics Research Institute (PRI)
Deliverable D1.2 - Prototype WGM Cavities
Work Package 2 • Months 12-30

Acoustofluidic Integration & Particle Handling

Development of microfluidic channels integrated with interdigital transducers (IDTs) for precise acoustic focusing and single-cell alignment past the microlaser sensing nodes. Ensures laminar flow and minimal shear stress on fragile biological samples.

Lead Beneficiary BioFluidics Tech GmbH
Deliverable D2.2 - Acoustofluidic Chip v2
Work Package 3 • Months 18-36

AI-Powered Spectral Decoding & Mechanical Barcoding

Implementation of real-time convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and deep signal processing pipelines to analyze optical wavelength shifts and translate them instantly into mechanical barcodes for thousands of cells per second.

Lead Beneficiary European AI Lab & University of Technology
Deliverable D3.1 - Decoding Software Suite
Work Package 4 • Months 30-48

Clinical Validation & Industrial Demonstration

Benchmarking the fully integrated -BARC device on live cancer cell lines and clinical blood samples. Validation against standard gold-standard flow cytometry and atomic force microscopy to confirm diagnostic efficacy and reliability.

Lead Beneficiary Medical Oncology Research Center
Deliverable D4.3 - Final Clinical Trial Report
