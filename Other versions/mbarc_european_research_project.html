Technology

Opto-Acoustic Resonance Architecture

Discover how MBARC couples whispering-gallery mode (WGM) microlasers with high-frequency surface acoustic waves.

Whispering-Gallery Mode Cavities

Light circulates along the interior perimeter of ring or disk microresonators through total internal reflection. When a cell or particle passes near the evanescent field, minute mechanical deformations shift the resonant wavelength with sub-picometer precision.

  • Quality factor Q > 10^6
  • Silicon-on-Insulator & polymer platforms
  • Immune to electromagnetic interference
Acoustofluidic Manipulation

Using interdigital transducers (IDTs) integrated into the microfluidic chip, acoustic radiation forces align, focus, and subject cells to controlled mechanical perturbations right at the optical sensing junction.

  • Frequency range: 500 MHz to 2 GHz
  • Laminar flow velocity control
  • Zero thermal damage to biological samples
Work Packages

Project Structure & Roadmap

Explore the 4 core technical work packages guiding MBARC from foundational nanofabrication to clinical trials.

Work Package 1 • Months 1-18 Lead: ETH Zurich

Advanced Microlaser Fabrication & Cavity Optimization

Design and nanofabrication of high-Q whispering-gallery mode microlasers. This pillar focuses on optimizing resonance linewidths and tuning emission spectra to maximize sensitivity against acoustic pressure variations in cellular assays.

Key Deliverable: D1.2 - Prototype WGM Cavities Completed (Month 12)
Work Package 2 • Months 12-30 Lead: BioFluidics GmbH

Acoustofluidic Integration & Particle Handling

Development of microfluidic channels integrated with interdigital transducers (IDTs) for precise acoustic focusing and single-cell alignment past the microlaser sensing nodes with minimal shear stress.

Key Deliverable: D2.2 - Acoustofluidic Chip v2 In Progress (Month 24)
Work Package 3 • Months 18-36 Lead: AI Lab & Tech Univ

AI-Powered Spectral Decoding & Mechanical Barcoding

Implementation of real-time convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and deep signal processing pipelines to analyze optical wavelength shifts and translate them instantly into mechanical barcodes for thousands of cells per second.

Key Deliverable: D3.1 - Decoding Software Suite Upcoming
Work Package 4 • Months 30-48 Lead: Karolinska Institute

Clinical Validation & Industrial Demonstration

Benchmarking the fully integrated MBARC device on live cancer cell lines and clinical blood samples against standard flow cytometry and atomic force microscopy.

Key Deliverable: D4.3 - Final Clinical Trial Report Scheduled Month 46
