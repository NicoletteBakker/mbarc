What MBARC is

Today, tracking individual cells within a sample requires labels that alter them or that blur together. MBARC proposes another route: using each cell's mechanical properties as its identity, read with laser light at the micrometre scale.

Microlasers

Tiny resonators that emit light at very precise wavelengths, inside or next to the cell.

Acoustic sensing

The cell's mechanical vibrations shift the laser spectrum and leave a measurable fingerprint.

Barcode

That fingerprint becomes a unique code that lets you identify and follow each cell or particle.
