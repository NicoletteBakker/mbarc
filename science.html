Scientific BACKGROUND of M-BARC

Every cell has a mechanical identity: its stiffness and elasticity reflect its health, development and response to its environment, often before a microscope or chemical test can detect any change. M-BARC reads these properties as a “barcode” to tell cells and particles apart, focusing on embryo viability in IVF, antibiotic resistance in bacteria, and drug responses in 3D spheroids and organoids, opening a new, label-free way to understand living systems.
